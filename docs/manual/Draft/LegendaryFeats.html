Legendary Feats Draft

At level 60, characters who have remained in a single pure class are granted a pool of Legendary Feats via the server HAK. These feats represent the ultimate mastery of a chosen path — abilities no multiclassed build can replicate. Each class receives 2 picks from the pool (Fighter receives 3), gated by sensible prerequisites so players can personalise their build rather than being locked into one archetype.

High magic server. These feats are intentionally strong. The server is tuned for epic-level, high-magic play and these bonuses reflect that. They are rewards for commitment to a pure class, not balance patches.

Draft. All feats on this page are under active design review. Names, effects, and prerequisites may change before implementation. Do not rely on this page for final balance decisions.

Overview

How the legendary feat system works.

When a character reaches level 60 with no levels in any other class, the HAK grants a Pure <Classname> feat automatically. This feat unlocks the legendary feat pool for that class and allows the player to select their allotted picks from the lists below. Prerequisites are checked at the time of selection. Picks are permanent and cannot be changed after resting.

Implementation note Legendary feats are granted via HAK feat and managed through local variables. Ability score bonuses are applied as item properties on an undroppable cursed token. Spell upgrades are handled by branching in the relevant spell scripts when a flag variable is set on the caster.

Dominion Feats

Damage type override for full 9-spell-level casters only. Toggle via cantrip during combat.

Dominion feats allow a caster to override the damage type of all their damaging spells to a chosen element. The active element is selected by casting a specific cantrip, consuming one level 0 spell slot. The selection persists until switched or until rest. With no toggle cast, spells behave normally.

Eligibility. Dominion feats require access to 9th level spells and are therefore restricted to pure Wizard, Sorcerer, Cleric, and Druid builds. Paladin, Bard, and Ranger are excluded by design and receive separate hybrid caster feats instead.

Toggle system Each cantrip toggle consumes one level 0 slot. A caster with 10 level 0 slots may switch element up to 10 times per rest. In practice most players commit to 1–2 elements per dungeon. No toggle = default spell damage types.
