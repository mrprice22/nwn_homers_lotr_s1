Quest Guide — Homer's LOTR

This guide covers every quest in the module: who gives it, where to find them, what you have to do, and what you walk away with. Start with the summary table below, then jump to any quest's card for the full walkthrough.

A note on what's finished. Everything listed here is live in the module, but a few quests are still being polished and one or two may not be fully implemented yet. Anything outstanding is tracked publicly on the Roadmap, under Needs Manual Finishing.

Daily and weekly quests reset on the real-world clock at midnight UTC (weeklies at Monday 00:00 UTC). Every repeatable quest-giver will quote you the exact time remaining if you come back early, so you never have to guess.

Low-Level Quests

One-off adventures for characters finding their feet.

Bill Ferny's Ring

Giver: Bill Ferny, Bree Level range: 1–10 Reward: 500 XP + 200 gp (or 500 XP + keep Sharkey's Orders)

Bill Ferny, a shifty resident of Bree, claims an underling ran into a nearby cave with his ring. He asks you to retrieve it. A straightforward early quest that uncovers a darker conspiracy.

How to start: Speak to Bill Ferny in Bree.

Steps:
  1. Accept Ferny's request.
  2. Enter Bree Cave and recover the Ring of Sharkey from the bandits (or a container).
  3. Return to Ferny. The ring's inscription reads "Servant of Sharkey" — suspicious.
  4. At Ferny's house you will face an ambush. Two choices after:
    • Give Ferny back his letter (Sharkey's Orders) — 500 XP + 200 gp.
    • Keep the letter — 500 XP, no gold, but you retain an evidence item.

Do Ferny's Return first and Ferny pays you half again as much here — an extra 100 gp on either ending.

Journal entries:
  1. You agreed to help Ferny recover his ring from a bandit who fled into a nearby cave.
  2. You delivered the ring. The inscription "Servant of Sharkey" gives you pause.
  3. An ambush awaited at Ferny's house. You kept the letter about Sharkey.
  4. An ambush awaited at Ferny's house. You gave Ferny his letter back.

Ferny's Return

Giver: Guardian of Bree, Bree Level range: ~3 (one-off) Reward: 250 XP + 200 gp + Ferny's Filching Gloves (Pick Pocket +2)

A prequel to Bill Ferny's Ring. Bill Ferny has been seen skulking around his old house — but Ferny himself has been standing in the market all the while. Someone is wearing Ferny's name, and the Bree watch is stretched too thin to find out who.

How to start: Ask any Guardian of Bree (the town guards) if anything strange is going on in town.

Steps:
  1. Accept the guard's request. The impostor takes up residence in Bill Ferny's house.
  2. Confront the "Bill Ferny" squatting there and call his bluff. He is one of Sharkey's ruffians, and he offers a choice:
    • Take his bribe — 75 gp from Ferny's own stash, and he slips out of Bree; or
    • Refuse and fight — he turns hostile.
  3. Report back to any Guardian of Bree for the payout — the report line differs by outcome, but both complete the quest.

Prequel bonus: completing this quest permanently unlocks a grateful greeting from Bill Ferny and a half-again payout on the Ferny's Ring turn-in (+100 gp on either ending). The favour sticks across relogs and reboots.

The Miller's Son

Giver: Han the Bree Miller, Bree Level range: 1–10 Reward: 450 XP

The Miller of Bree has lost his son — the boy was delivering an ale shipment to Tharbad along the South Greenway Road. He asks you to find out what happened.

How to start: Speak to Han the Bree Miller in Bree.

Steps:
  1. Accept the Miller's request.
  2. Travel south along the Greenway. You will find what happened to his son.
  3. Return and deliver the grim news to the Miller — 450 XP.
Journal entries:
  1. You agreed to find the Miller's son and the missing ale shipment headed for Tharbad.
  2. You delivered the sad news. The Miller grieves; his wife and his ale are small comfort.

Finishing this unlocks the sequel, The Miller's Other Son.

The Miller's Other Son

Giver: Han the Bree Miller, Bree Level range: ~8 (one-off) Reward: 500 XP + 500 gp + Magic Bag (good endings) / 200 XP (sad truth)

A sequel to The Miller's Son — offered only after that quest is complete. Han's other son, Dob, took his brother's death hard and fell in with a "trading crowd" on the road to Tharbad. The traders turned out to be cultists of the Red Eye, and their leader — a man they call the Voice — keeps the boy close.

How to start: Speak to Han the Bree Miller in Bree after completing The Miller's Son.

Steps:
  1. Accept Han's plea.
  2. (Optional) Talk to Tolly the Peddler at Tharbad Bridge — a coin (10 gp) or a kind word buys directions to the cult camp.
  3. Confront the Voice of the Red Eye in Tharbad (East). Three resolutions:
    • Persuade (DC 18, one attempt per character, ever): he releases Dob peacefully. On a failed roll he attacks;
    • Fight — he turns hostile; his death frees the boy (and advances all party members in the area);
    • Walk away — leave Dob to the cult. You can still change your mind and fight him later.
  4. Report back to Han — outcome-specific lines. Freed endings pay 500 gp + 500 XP + a Magic Bag ("Dob's trading pack"); the sad truth pays 200 XP.

Mid-Level Quests

Longer journeys for seasoned adventurers.

Feeding Tharbad

Giver: Temple of Ilúvatar cleric, Tharbad Level range: 5–15 Reward: 500 XP + Book of the Cora

The Temple of Ilúvatar in Tharbad is struggling to feed hungry refugees at the bridge. They ask you to purchase venison from a local store and hand it out to 20 refugees at the bridge.

How to start: Speak to the Temple of Ilúvatar cleric in Tharbad.

Steps:
  1. Agree to help.
  2. Buy venison from the general store.
  3. Travel to Tharbad Bridge and feed 20 refugees.
  4. Return to the cleric — 500 XP and the Book of the Cora awarded.
Journal entries:
  1. You agreed to help the Temple feed hungry refugees at Tharbad Bridge — buy venison and feed 20 people.
  2. The refugees will not go hungry tonight. You gave selflessly for a noble cause.

Glorfindel's Curative

Giver: Glorfindel, Rivendell Level range: mid Reward: 1,700 XP + Bracers of Glorfindel

Glorfindel of Rivendell asks you to gather the ingredients for a curative: Athelas from the foothills of the Misty Mountains, a graveside token from the Statue of Arathorn headstone in the Barrow Downs, and drake essence from the Caves of the Carrock.

How to start: Speak to Glorfindel in Rivendell.

Steps:
  1. Accept the task.
  2. Gather the three ingredients across the Misty-Mountain foothills, the Barrow Downs, and the Caves of the Carrock.
  3. Return to Glorfindel for 1,700 XP and the Bracers of Glorfindel.

Full write-up: Glorfindel's Curative.

The Twentieth Plot of Mazarbul

Giver: Frár the Restless, a shade of Balin's colony, in the Chamber of Records (Moria) Level range: 18+ (one-off) Reward: 1200 gp + 2000 XP + Seal of the Twentieth (Damage Reduction +1/Soak 5, Damage Resistance: Negative 10/-)

A hidden codicil to the Book of Mazarbul names twenty burial plots whose seal-fires keep the dead of Moria at rest. Nineteen were lit before the colony fell — the twentieth never was, and what lies beneath it does not lie still. Frár, a restless dwarf-shade among the toppled record-stones, asks you to finish his colony's last labor: light the three crypt-seal braziers (one in the Chamber of Records, two in Balin's Tomb), then destroy the wraith that rises to smother the third flame.

How to start: Speak to Frár the Restless in the Chamber of Records; level 18+ required, once per character (one-off — the seal, once set, holds).

Steps:
  1. Accept Frár's charge — the journal opens and the seal-braziers will take your flame.
  2. Use each of the three Crypt-Seal Braziers once (per-character, persistent — floaty text counts "N of 3"; each lighting also grants a short ward against grave-chill, Negative resist 5/- for 5 minutes).
  3. The third brazier raises the Wraith of the Twentieth Plot (CR 21). Destroy it — the killing blow's party credits every party member in the area who is at the wraith stage. If the wraith is slain while you are absent (or you fall and return), stir any brazier's flame to re-summon it, so the fight can always be re-staged.
  4. Return to Frár: 1200 gp, 2000 XP and the Seal of the Twentieth. The quest closes for good.

Elrond's Request

Giver: Elrond, Rivendell Level range: 15–30 Reward: 2,000 XP + Elrond's Writ

Elrond of Rivendell charges you with two tasks: break the Uruk-hai siege of the Troll Halls and retrieve the Helm of Azog the Warlord, then venture into Moria and find evidence of Balin's Expedition (the Dwarf Head). This is the module's main mid-game quest chain.

How to start: Speak to Elrond in Rivendell and accept both tasks.

Steps:
  1. Kill Azog the Warlord (CR 14) in the Angmar Mountain area and loot the Helm of the Warlord.
  2. Push into Balin's Tomb in Moria and retrieve the Dwarf Head (evidence of Balin's expedition).
  3. Return to Elrond with both items in your party's inventory.
  4. Agree to accept your reward — both items are consumed, and you gain 2,000 XP and Elrond's Writ.
Journal entries:
  1. Elrond has given you two tasks: break the Uruk-hai siege of the Trollshaws and bring the Helm of Azog; venture into Moria and find sign of Balin's Expedition.
  2. Both tasks are complete. You now have a new place to call home.

High-Level Quests

The great deeds, for characters at the height of their power.

Ruin of Annuminas

Giver: Hanee the Loon, an old woman in Bree Level range: 25+ Reward: 5,000 XP (bring Azagoth's Head back to Hanee)

An old woman in Bree tells you to seek out the Ruins of Annuminas, an ancient Dúnedain realm to the north. This is the first stage of a two-part arc: kill Azagoth in the ruins, bring his head back to Hanee for 5,000 XP (she doesn't take it — she gleefully points you to the wizards in Gondor), then deliver the head to the Gondor Scribe in the Tower of the High Wizard for a further 10,000 XP.

How to start: Speak to Hanee the Loon in Bree.

Steps:
  1. Hear Hanee out and head north to the Ruins of Annuminas.
  2. Find and kill Azagoth (CR 37) and loot Azagoth's Head.
  3. Return to Hanee with the head still in hand for 5,000 XP — then carry it on to The Well of Souls.

The Well of Souls

Giver: the Gondor Scribe, Tower of the High Wizard: Ground Floor Level range: 30–45 Reward: 10,000 XP + Annuminas Key

The Gondor Scribe asks you to face Azagoth, who threatens to open the Well of Souls — kill Azagoth in the Ruins of Annuminas (not Moria!) and bring back his head to prove the deed is done. This is the second stage of the Ruin of Annuminas arc.

How to play:
  1. Hanee the Loon in Bree points you north to the Ruins of Annuminas.
  2. Find and kill Azagoth (CR 37) in the Ruins of Annuminas and loot Azagoth's Head.
  3. Return to Hanee for an intermediate 5,000 XP; she keeps you holding the head and points you to the Gondor wizards.
  4. Take the head to the Gondor Scribe in the Tower of the High Wizard: Ground Floor — she consumes the head and awards 10,000 XP and the Annuminas Key.
  5. Alternate start: talk to the scribe without the head and she offers the quest directly (with the key up front), then kill Azagoth and return for the turn-in.

The Ruins of Annuminas hold several old chests. Most can still be picked open by a skilled hand, but two are magically warded — the Sorcerer's Warded Coffer and the Wizard's Sealed Reliquary — and no lockpick will ever move them. Only the Annuminas Key turns their locks, and the key crumbles to nothing the moment it is used, so you may claim the contents of only one of the two warded chests. Each holds three pieces of potent caster gear: the coffer rewards sorcerers (Charisma, innate spell slots, elemental resistances), the reliquary rewards wizards (Intelligence, memorized spell slots, resistances). Choose the set that suits your caster. Take the scribe's offer to receive the key.

Journal entries:
  1. You agreed to face Azagoth and stop his entry into the Well of Souls. May fate guide your steps.
  2. Azagoth is dead and the Well of Souls is safe. Time for a drink at the Prancing Pony.

Gloison's Heirloom

Giver: Gloin King Under the Mountain, Erebor (Lonely Mountain: Main Hall) Level range: high (Gerrey is CR 17) Reward: 5,000 XP + 15,000 gp

Glóin asks you to recover his kinsman Gloisin's family heirloom — a coat of arms stolen by the brigand Gerrey Halliams, who fled to the Ruins of Dale.

How to start: Speak to Gloin in Erebor and accept the task.

Steps:
  1. Accept the task from Gloin.
  2. Travel to the Ruins of Dale and kill Gerrey Halliams (CR 17). He carries Gloison's Coat of Arms as droppable loot.
  3. Return to Gloin holding the heirloom — the turn-in only appears while you have it. He takes it and pays 5,000 XP + 15,000 gp.

Paths of the Dead

Giver: Aragorn, at the gates of Minas Tirith Level range: high (end-game) Reward: Andúril, Flame of the West

Aragorn summons you to walk the Paths of the Dead: rouse the Oathbreakers under the mountain, hold to the oath sworn of old, and turn the tide against the Witch-king. A full five-stage quest chain and the module's marquee reward — a copy of Andúril.

How to start: Speak to Aragorn at the gates of Minas Tirith.

Steps:
  1. Accept Aragorn's charge.
  2. Seek the King of the Dead in the Dimholt / Dunharrow.
  3. Fight through the Oathbreaker trials.
  4. Claim the reward — Andúril, Flame of the West.
  5. The chain completes back at Dunharrow.

Full write-up: Paths of the Dead.

Legendary Quests

End-game content for characters who have run out of things to be afraid of. More legendary-tier lines are on the Roadmap.

The Forbidden Realms

Giver: none — the quest starts the moment you come by the Forbidden Realms Key Where: the key is carried by Summanus in Númenor: Noirinan; the gate it opens is in the same area Level range: end-game — the barrow-court is among the hardest content in the module Reward: whatever the Weathertop King, Queen and Archer are carrying, and the run of a tomb nobody has been inside

The Forbidden Realms Key has been stealable from Summanus for as long as anyone can remember, and until now it opened precisely nothing. It opens something now. It is a black thing of Númenórean make, heavier than its size wants to be, and the seven marks cut along its ward are not house-wards or chest-wards — they are grave-wards, the sort the old kings cut into a door they did not intend anyone to open again.

Somewhere in the drowned country there is a stone that answers it: a slab of grey stone set into the hillside of Noirinan, with no handle, no hinge and no keyhole, and the same seven marks on its face. Put the key to it and the seal gives way onto the Tombs of the Lost Souls — a barrow that is not a ruin but a court, laid out for the dead the way a hall is laid out for the living. Its lords have not gone quiet. The Weathertop King, his Queen and their Archer still hold their places, and they were sealed in for a reason.

How to start: Get the Forbidden Realms Key — pickpocket it from Summanus, loot it, or take it in trade. Any route works; the journal opens the moment it is in your pack. If you stole it long ago and still carry it, it will catch up with you the next time you log in.

Steps:
  1. Come by the key. The journal opens with what the seven marks mean.
  2. Find the seal in Noirinan and use it while carrying the key. Without the key the stone simply describes itself to you and stays shut.
  3. Take the barrow-court. Three end-game undead, all of them the sort of fight you bring a party to. Bring one.
  4. Get out. The gate stands on the tomb side as well and does not ask for the key on the way back, so losing it in there does not entomb you.

The court re-forms once the tomb has been cleared and left empty, so the fight is repeatable — but the journal completes once, the first time one character has all three down. Party members who are in the tomb when a court member falls all get the credit.

Recurring Quests

Daily, weekly and repeatable work. Every giver will quote you the time remaining if you come back early.

The Last Drop at Frogmorton Inn

Giver: Rigrin, the Whitfurrow Inn Level range: any (daily, pure social) Reward: 25 gp + Frogmorton Pint (heals 4 hp, brief tipsy wobble)

The last cask of Frogmorton's famous harvest ale — the Floating Log's own autumn brew, carted over from Frogmorton before the brewer died — sits in Rigrin's cellar, and five patrons each claim the final drop. Rigrin judges the matter by the custom of the cask, an old rule that turns with the month, and he'll pay a neutral party to hear the claimants out and name the rightful drinker. No combat; pure Shire-tone dialogue.

How to start: Ask Rigrin at the Whitfurrow Inn (one area over from Frogmorton) what the common-room row is about.

Steps:
  1. Rigrin states this month's custom of the cask (e.g. "the coin that bought the barrel drains it") — twelve rules cycling through the year.
  2. Chat with the five claimants in the common room — Marigold Bunce (brewed it), Poppy Thistlewool (paid for it), Hob Broadbelt (tapped it and drank first), Ned Puddifoot (hauled it from Frogmorton), Daisy Goodbody (won it at darts) — and work out who the rule points at.
  3. Give Rigrin your answer. One judgment per day: a right call pays 25 gp plus a Frogmorton Pint; a wrong call gets you gently laughed out of the room — either way the matter is settled until tomorrow.

The Frogmorton Pint is a single-use drink: 4 hp back and a short, purely cosmetic drunken stagger. It cannot hurt you or anyone else.

Hobbit Post

Giver: Posco Whitfoot, post-master, Hobbiton Level range: all levels (daily, no combat) Reward: 60 gp base + 40 gp speed bonus + 50 XP; misdelivered parcels pay 60 gp extra and a small loot item

Posco Whitfoot has founded the Hobbit Post and needs an honest pair of legs: one sealed parcel per real day, delivered somewhere in the Shire for pay. The addressee rotates with the calendar day across five recipients — Daddy Twofoot (Hobbiton), Old Noakes (Bywater), the Keeper of the Green Dragon (Green Dragon Inn, door off Hobbiton), and Salvia Brockhouse and Milo Burrows (both in the Whitfurrow Inn common room). Pure low-pressure Shire flavour; no combat.

How to start: Talk to Posco Whitfoot in Hobbiton and take today's parcel.

Steps:
  1. Posco reads you the address off the label (it rotates daily). Forgot where it's bound? Ask him again — he'll re-read the label, or write a fresh one.
  2. Carry the parcel to the named recipient and talk to them. The Shire judges its post by the freshness of the seal: land it within 5 real minutes of accepting and the fee rises from 60 gp to 100 gp.
  3. Rarely (8%) Posco's label is wrong: the named recipient refuses the parcel and points you at its true owner (another of the five), who pays 60 gp extra plus a small bonus item for untangling the post.
  4. Payment on delivery closes the day; the post-bag reopens at UTC midnight (Posco quotes the exact time remaining).

The parcel itself is plot-flagged and cursed — it can't be sold, dropped or traded, and re-accepting from Posco replaces any parcel you're already carrying, so they can never pile up.

Spider Silk Harvest

Giver: Thranduil, King of Eryn Lasgalen, Thranduil's Hall (through the Wood of Legolas, off Mirkwood: North) Level range: 12+ (daily kill-collect) Reward: 300 gp + 600 XP per day

The great spiders choke Thranduil's wood, but their silk is stronger than steel wire and his weavers prize it. Once per real day the Elvenking pays a flat bounty for six bolts of spider silk cut from the spiders of Mirkwood. How to start: Enter Bree Cave and speak to the Whispering Wretch; accept the challenge. Open to all levels.

How to start: Travel to the Ruins of Annuminas as a character with at least one Sorcerer level and speak to Erendis of the Drowned House.

Steps:
  1. Node 1 (Sorcerer 1): Let her name what woke in you — she puts a hand on your breastbone and says it out loud in Adunaic, which she insists is not a sentence and not a gift but a fact, like your height. The journal opens and she works the Signet of the Drowned House (+1 CHA, Sorcerer-only) off her own hand into yours. Not an heirloom; she keeps a box of them. It only means somebody knew and did not run. Come back at Sorcerer 8.
  2. Node 2 (Sorcerer 8): Return; Erendis gives you the Shard of Sea-Glass, a worn scrap off a window in Armenelos that has never once done anything — which is the point of it. It is not a charm, it is a bill that has not been presented. Carry it, don’t stare at it, and bring it back at Sorcerer 15.
  3. Node 3 (Sorcerer 15): Say the price out loud, in the words you would use while still angry about it — she has no use for the tidied version. She sets the shard into the head of a black lebethron staff she has evidently had ready for some time, and the Staff of Elder Days (+2 / +1 CHA quarterstaff, Sorcerer-only) is yours. The quest closes; the longer roads are named for a later chunk.

Both rewards sit inside Sorcerer proficiency, so nothing on this line ships unusable. The level gates count Sorcerer levels only, not your total character level. One-off per character — the stages only ever move forward, so the rewards can’t be farmed.

Prestige Order Trials

Halmir the Grey in the Well of Eru opens the twelve order initiations.

Prestige-Order Hub (Halmir the Grey)

NPC: Halmir the Grey, Keeper of the Old Orders, at the Well of Eru Level range: order lines unlock at L6/11/12/13/14/16 Reward: opens all twelve prestige-order trials below

An old man in a grey cloak keeps the rolls of the twelve old orders — the prestige classes, framed in-fiction as surviving disciplines of the elder days (Harper Scouts, Knights of Westernesse, Pale Masters, Shadowdancers, Arcane Archers, Red Dragon Disciples, Assassins, Weapon Masters, Blackguards, Divine Champions, Dwarven Defenders, Shifters). Ask him "Which of the old orders would hear my name as I stand?" and he lists every order your character currently qualifies for. Each qualifying order also gets its own dialogue branch with an evocative pitch — and, for all twelve, an initiation trial you can take on the spot.

How it works:
  1. Order lines appear only when you meet that order's minimum character level (Harper Scout / Knight of Westernesse 6+, Pale Master / Shadowdancer 11+, Arcane Archer / Red Dragon Disciple 12+, Assassin / Weapon Master 13+, Blackguard / Divine Champion / Dwarven Defender 14+, Shifter 16+). The Dwarven Defenders additionally require a dwarf.
  2. Halmir's summary line lists all currently qualifying orders; below level 6 it says none would hear your name yet.
  3. Each order's trial additionally requires at least one level in that prestige class — Halmir points the unqualified toward the discipline rather than the roll.
  4. The hub itself has no journal entry and no reward; the twelve trials below carry those.

The Cipher in the Inn (Harper Scout initiation)

Giver: Halmir the Grey, Well of Eru (Harper branch) Requires: 1+ Harper Scout level (the hub line itself shows at character level 6+) Reward: 750 XP + Harper Pin (Listen +3, Spot +3, Lore +3)

The first of the twelve prestige-order trials. Ask Halmir of the Harper Scouts: if you already walk their roads, he sends you to a friend who keeps the corner table at the Prancing Pony in Bree — Della Heathertoes, a traveller in a fern-green cloak. She tests you with a line of old road-cant, each word written tail-first: DAOR TSEW EHT SEHCTAW YERG EHT. A learned character (Lore rank 8+) reads it at a glance; anyone else gets her hint and picks the true reading from three (wrong picks loop back to the hint — wordier, never a dead end). Read it true and she trusts you with the counter-word; carry it back to Halmir and he enters your name on the rolls, with the pin to prove it. One-off per character.

Steps:
  1. Speak to Halmir, choose the Harper Scouts, accept the errand.
  2. Sit with Della at the Prancing Pony; solve the cipher via the Lore shortcut or the hint path.
  3. Return to Halmir and say the word — the XP and the pin are yours, and the quest closes.
