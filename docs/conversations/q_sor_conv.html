entry #0 (if q_sor_c_off) ¶
*The woman at the fire looks up without any surprise at all, the way people do when they have been watching you cross open ground for ten minutes.* Sit down if you like. It is a public fire, more or less. You have the look. I do not mean the robes. There is a pressure that comes off people whose power was not given to them by anybody -- no order, no god, no teacher who could take it away again if you disappointed him. It simply woke up in you one day like a second heartbeat, and you have been carrying it since and mostly pretending it is under control. I am Erendis. My people drowned. What is left of them is standing in front of me being polite. *She turns her hand over so the grey ring catches the fire.* I cannot teach you a spell -- nobody taught me one, that is the whole difficulty of our sort. So I will give you the only piece of it that hands on. Stop fighting it. Not indulge it; stop *fighting* it. Half of what a young sorcerer calls losing control is the blood shoving back against a hand held over its mouth. Take the hand away and it goes quiet and does as it is told, which is more frightening and a great deal safer. Come to the fire and let me say what you are out loud. It will be the first time anyone has.
reply #0 → q_sor_lrn ¶

entry #7 ¶
*She stands up, puts a hand flat on your breastbone, and says your name and one other word in Adunaic, quite quietly, the way you would read a label.* There. That is what you are. It only means somebody knew and did not run. Go and be eight levels deeper into it than you are, and I will still be here. The lake is slow.

entry #6 ¶

I am Erendis, and this heap of stones was the capital of the world once, which nobody believes until they are standing in it. No, I do not tell fortunes. I sit with sorcerers. If you are not one, you are welcome to the fire and to the story of how Numenor went under, which is long and has no moral in it whatsoever.
