*He tips the harp toward you in something that is not quite a bow.* The Lay is out of my keeping and going about on a sword-edge, which is a stranger fate than I intended for it and a better one. Sing it badly in bad inns; that is how a thing survives. There are longer roads in this craft — the naming-songs, the lament that stops a fight, the making of a true name that a thing cannot refuse — but those are for a later evening than this one.
