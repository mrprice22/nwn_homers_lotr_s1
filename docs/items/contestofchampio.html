The Contest of Champions will be a series of tournament duels to be held at the Tharbad Arena. *** Dueling Categories *** When you apply to enter the Contest, you shall fight others of a similar level range and of similar class. However the winners of the bouts go on to fight others of their class until each class has one champion. Those champions are assured a prize from this point on. *** The Main Event *** Once we have a champion from each class, these champions will face off against one another. This end victor will be crowned Supreme Chamion of the Land. And a special custom prize unique to only them will be awarded. *** How to apply *** To enter, you must pay to a DM the entry fee of 100GP per level. So if you are level 5 it shall cost 500 GP to enter. At that time you will be given a Contest Token. Do not loose this, and do not give this to others. It is bound to you personally. In addition, if you have multiple characters, you can sign them up as well. *** A Word on Multiclass *** If you are a multi-class character we shall assign you to a class division based on your most prominant class. *** Rules and Regulations *** At the beginning of each bout, you must Salute your opponent and then the judge. Everything you walk in with is allowable for the match. You will be disqualified if you leave or step outside the fight area. You can yeild the match if you so choose. Simply shout "I Yield". The judges will ressurect fallen duelists. *** How to win a match *** A match will be decided in your favor if you slay your opponent, they yield, or by judges decision. GOOD LUCK TO ALL
