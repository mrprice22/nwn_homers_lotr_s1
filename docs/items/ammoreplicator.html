A deep elven quiver of oiled leather, its mouth stitched with a knotwork that does not quite hold still. Reach into it holding a fletcher's measure in mind and the quiver will match it, shaft for shaft, until the pattern is spent. Activate the quiver and choose one kind of arrow, bolt or bullet you are carrying; 500 more of that same ammunition are drawn out of it. The weave holds only two such workings. After the second, the quiver crumbles to dust.
