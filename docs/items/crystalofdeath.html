This is the Crystal Shard of Death. Though these crystals were rumors it seems that the legends were true. If this crystal exists than this means that the legend of the other elemental pieces lie scattered somwhere. With this Crystal you can track the direction of the other crystals. Though a fair warningto those who find em. As others are also looking for em, for reasons that only legends can tell as everyone has forgotten the purpose of these Crystalls. Maybe the elves would know as they have been on this world even before the first Human true settlement. These is the Crystal of Death, its power corrupts all that touches it unless it is someone that shares the crystals focus. (Evil or Neutral). And only spell casters can use this item to it's full potential as it requires someone sensitive to magic to trigger it's effects. This crystal is highly prized by necromancers. Though this crystal is evil it's counter part the Crystal of Life is able to counter it's effects.
