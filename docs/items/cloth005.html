Developed by Lantanese spelunkers, this type of silk vest has been coated by a slick, oily substance allowing the wearer to slip through small crevices -- and incidentally making it very difficult to restrain the wearer in any way. Calmishite thieves quickly adopted the vest, and now sets of magically enhanced lock picks are commonly found in secret pockets throughout the vest.
