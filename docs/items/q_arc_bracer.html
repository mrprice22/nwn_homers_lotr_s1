Grey leather bracers stitched with a single feather in silver thread, given by Halmir the Grey to those who drew the warden's shaft from the old mark of Imladris bow in hand and arrow nocked. Wearing them, the world goes quiet the way it does at full draw: the eye finds the far thing, the ear the small one, and the breath keeps its own steady count.
